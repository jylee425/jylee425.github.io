Juyong Lee


I am a PhD(/MS int.) candidate at KAIST, advised by Kimin Lee. I received a B.S. degree with a double major in both mathematics and computer science/engineering at POSTECH. I have an experience as an exchange student at Stanford. I am currently a research engineer at Google DeepMind, reporting to Daniel Toyama.

My doctoral research focuses on developing autonomous agents for controlling mobile devices with language models. Currently, I am interested in enabling agents to operate over extremely long horizons.
